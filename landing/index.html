The hidden curriculum

The rules nobody wrote down.

Kids with white-collar parents absorb these for free. Everyone else finds out the hard way — after the rejection. Here's a few we teach on day one.

i.

Recruiters skim for numbers, not adjectives. "Hard-working" says nothing; "cut response time 30%" says everything.

ii.

"Responsible for" describes a chair. Lead with the result you produced.

iii.

The exact skills in the job post are the words they'll search for. Mirror the ones you really have.

iv.

Most of your score comes from things you can fix today — not from the degree you don't have.
